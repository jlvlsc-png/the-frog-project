Behind The Vision

John Lloyd N. Velasco

A dedicated BS-Architecture student from ISU-I, John Lloyd experienced firsthand the bottlenecks of traditional architectural programming, site analysis, and material computation.

Rather than accepting the status quo, he developed The Frog Project. The goal was simple: engineer an ecosystem of tools that act as a force multiplier for architects and builders. Let the software handle the heavy lifting, ensuring accuracy and speed, so professionals can focus on pure design and execution.
