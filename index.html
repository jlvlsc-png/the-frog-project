Behind The Vision

John Lloyd N. Velasco

He developed The Frog Project, an ecosystem of smart and multipurpose tools for Students, Architects, Engineers, Builders, etc. . The goal was simple : Engineer an ecosystem of smart tools that acts as a force multiplier for architects and builders. Let the software handle the heavy lifting, ensure the accuracy and speed while working, so professionals can focus on pure design and execution.
